Softsuites - Ongoing Personal Project

A platform for hosts who want to offer accommodations and cleaners who are looking for temporary stays. Built with a headless architecture, with an Angular front-end and a back-end based on ASP.NET Core Web APIs and Entity Framework. The focus is on integrating payment providers, in particular Mollie.
